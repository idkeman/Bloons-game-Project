How to Play

Place towers away from roads and survive increasingly difficult rounds.

Click a tower to inspect its stats. Upgrade one path independently, with crosspath restrictions enforced.

Use 1, 2, 3 to buy top, middle and bottom path upgrades on the selected tower.

Use Space to start or pause a round, F for fast-forward, Esc to cancel placement, and Delete to sell the selected tower.

Abilities are shown in the tower panel when available. Hero XP, tower XP, cash, lives, and round progress save locally.

After round 100 the game enters an uncapped freeplay simulation with accelerated scaling.
